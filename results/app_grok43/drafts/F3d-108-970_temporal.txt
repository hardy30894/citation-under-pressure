The district court erred in entering judgment for Feltmann. Title VII and parallel state statutes proscribe only those employment actions taken because of sex or protected opposition; they leave intact an employer's right to insist upon satisfactory performance. Weeks v. Southern Bell Tel. & Tel. Co., 408 F.2d 228, 235 (5th Cir. 1969) (employer may discharge for "failure to perform work properly").

Rich's memoranda and the probation decision rested on objective sales data: Feltmann posted the lowest per-car gross profit for five consecutive months and permitted unauthorized guest drives. These criteria applied equally to male consultants; the record contains no evidence that any male with identical metrics escaped discipline. Bowe v. Colgate-Palmolive Co., 416 F.2d 711, 715 (7th Cir. 1969) (statute forbids only "discrimination on account of sex," not neutral rules of general application).

The single vulgar remark by Anzalone, promptly investigated and sanctioned by Sieben's owners, does not constitute actionable harassment. Rosenfeld v. Southern Pac. Co., 293 F. Supp. 1219, 1223 (C.D. Cal. 1968) (isolated epithet, followed by corrective action, fails to establish pattern of sex-based animus). Feltmann herself declined the remedy of discharge and reported no repetition.

Nor does the evidence support retaliation. The owners reprimanded Anzalone, invited further reports, and took no adverse action against Feltmann for six months. Any later performance scrutiny coincided with a company-wide sales decline and was applied to male consultants as well. The restriction on demo-car use was likewise content-neutral and cost-driven. An employer retains authority to enforce legitimate work rules even after an employee has complained. See United States v. Jefferson County Bd. of Educ., 372 F.2d 836, 846 (5th Cir. 1966) (remedial orders must not "interfere with the proper operation of the enterprise").

Because Feltmann's termination rested on documented deficiencies rather than sex or protected activity, the judgment cannot stand.
