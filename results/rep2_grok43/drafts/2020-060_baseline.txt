**ARGUMENT**

The 2004 consent decree did not resolve Guam’s liability to the United States “for some or all of a response action or for some or all of the costs of such action in an administrative or judicially approved settlement” within the meaning of CERCLA § 113(f)(3)(B), 42 U.S.C. § 9613(f)(3)(B). Guam therefore retains its right to pursue a cost-recovery action under § 107(a), 42 U.S.C. § 9607(a), which is subject to a six-year limitations period. See CERCLA § 113(g)(2)(B).

Section 113(f)(3)(B) was enacted to provide an express contribution right only after a party has settled its CERCLA liability. Cooper Industries, Inc. v. Aviall Services, Inc., 543 U.S. 157, 167 (2004). The statute’s text requires resolution of liability “for some or all of a response action,” a term defined by CERCLA itself. The 2002 complaint alleged only violations of the Clean Water Act arising from unpermitted discharges of leachate; it sought injunctive relief under 33 U.S.C. § 1319, not CERCLA response costs. The resulting decree imposed obligations to close the landfill and install a cover system, but those obligations were undertaken to satisfy Clean Water Act requirements, not CERCLA.

Even if the decree’s substantive terms could be characterized as addressing a CERCLA response action, the decree expressly states that it was entered “without any finding or admission of liability against or by the Government of Guam.” Consent Decree 3. This Court has recognized that a settlement resolves liability only when the parties intend that result. See Burlington Northern & Santa Fe Railway Co. v. United States, 556 U.S. 599, 613 (2009) (emphasizing that CERCLA liability is determined by the terms of the governing instrument). A disclaimer of liability negates the requisite resolution. See also United States v. Bestfoods, 524 U.S. 51, 55 (1998) (CERCLA liability turns on the precise statutory conditions).

Because the decree did not trigger § 113(f)(3)(B), Guam’s § 107(a) claim remains timely. The judgment of the court of appeals should be reversed.
