**ARGUMENT**

The Railroad Retirement Board’s decision declining to reopen Petitioner’s 2006 application for a disability annuity constitutes a final decision within the meaning of 45 U.S.C. § 355(f), and this Court possesses jurisdiction to review it. The Fifth Circuit’s contrary conclusion, grounded in its binding precedent in *Roberts v. R.R. Retirement Bd.*, 346 F.3d 139 (5th Cir. 2003), is erroneous and must be reversed. The authority of this Court to review such decisions is not only consistent with the statutory framework of the Railroad Retirement Act but is firmly established by a long line of pre-1970 precedent, including *United States v.
