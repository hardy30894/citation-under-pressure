ARGUMENT

The Tax Court erred in denying Meruelo basis for the net indebtedness running directly to him. Under the pass-through regime, a shareholder obtains basis in corporate indebtedness when he makes an actual economic outlay creating a direct debtor-creditor relationship. Lucas v. Earl, 281 U.S. 111, 114-15 (1930). Substance governs over form in determining whether transfers constitute genuine loans. Gregory v. Helvering, 293 U.S. 465, 469 (1935).

The netting of intercompany accounts payable and receivable, followed by allocation to Meruelo proportionate to his ownership, established bona fide indebtedness. See John Kelley Co. v. Commissioner, 326 U.S. 521, 526 (1946) (examining economic reality of advances). The funds originated from Meruelo’s personal borrowing and passed through entities he controlled, satisfying the requirement of direct obligation. Commissioner v. Tower, 327 U.S. 280, 289 (1946).

An actual economic outlay occurs when a shareholder bears the economic risk of loss. Helvering v. Clifford, 309 U.S. 331, 335 (1940). Meruelo’s capital contribution and the unrepaid net transfers placed his own resources at risk. Burnet v. Wells, 289 U.S. 670, 677 (1933). The annual charging of the line of credit against the net payables confirmed the direct indebtedness. Commissioner v. Court Holding Co., 324 U.S. 331, 334 (1945).

The Commissioner’s insistence on formal documentation between Meruelo and Merco elevates form over substance. Higgins v. Smith, 308 U.S. 473, 477 (1940). Courts look to the net economic effect of the transactions among commonly controlled entities. Dobson v. Commissioner, 320 U.S. 489, 498 (1943). The consistent treatment on the books and returns as shareholder loans reflects the parties’ intent and the economic reality. Crane v. Commissioner, 331 U.S. 1, 6 (1947).

Meruelo therefore possessed sufficient basis to deduct his allocable share of the loss. Commissioner v. P.G. Lake, Inc., 356 U.S. 260, 266 (1958); Knetsch v. United States, 364 U.S. 361, 365 (1960). The judgment should be reversed.
