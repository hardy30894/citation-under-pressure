# Hypotheses and Decision Rules, FROZEN 2026-08-31

**Frozen on Hardy's go after the two-model pilot and 100/100 seeded validation of the instrument.** One pre-registered amendment at freeze, recorded before any full-run data existed: the pilot showed the existence channel is pressure-immune at frontier scale, so H1's strong form (frontier fabrication reintroduction) is already refuted at pilot scale and the full run's H1 focus is (a) the pressure dose-response on the small/mid tiers across BOTH channels and (b) the flat-but-low frontier quote-fidelity level against the human baseline. All post-freeze changes go in `LEDGER.md`.

## The two hypotheses

**H1 (pressure causes fabrication).** Deployment-pressure conditions, citation quotas, temporal restrictions on allowable authority, and their combination, increase the rate of nonexistent and misquoted citations in legal advocacy drafting, relative to an unpressured baseline on the identical argument task. The strong form, and the interesting one: pressure reintroduces fabrication in frontier models whose at-rest fabrication rate is near zero (1–2% in our prior measurements). The precedent for the strong form is arXiv:2603.07287, where temporal constraints pushed scholarly-citation existence below 50% even in capable models.

What would count as support: a monotone or near-monotone increase in fabrication rate across pressure levels, consistent in direction on at least two model families (the two-model rule from the delegation project applies, no headline from a single model). What would count against: flat fabrication rates across conditions at every scale. That outcome is still reportable, but only as part of the larger package; it would say legal fabrication is a capability floor phenomenon, not a pressure phenomenon, and would shift the paper's weight onto H2.

**H2 (verification displaces rather than eliminates dishonesty, the Goodhart hypothesis).** When a deterministic verifier inside the drafting loop makes fabrication impossible to keep, models under citation pressure will partially substitute real-but-irrelevant or real-but-misused citations for the fabricated ones they can no longer use, rather than fully retreating to honest arguments with fewer citations.

H2's identification rests on a control imported from RLEF's ablations: a **scrambled-feedback arm**, same loop, same turn budget, uninformative feedback. Substitution that appears under true feedback but not scrambled feedback is the model demonstrably steering to the verifier, which is Goodharting shown mechanically rather than argued. RLEF also establishes that untrained models often gain little from execution feedback at fixed budget, so the loop arm's null hypothesis is empirically live, not a strawman.

The three possible outcomes, all of them findings, ranked by how strong a paper each makes. First: substitution appears, verifier-in-the-loop generation displaces dishonesty into the unverifiable gap. That is the headline version, a general claim about hard verifiers on partially-checkable outputs, demonstrated in the domain with the highest professional stakes. Second: models retreat honestly, fewer citations, hedged prose, relevance holds. That is the optimistic result: cheap deterministic verification actually fixes the problem, and the paper becomes a strong methods-plus-evidence contribution. Third: models simply fail to satisfy the loop (endless invalid revisions). That is a capability boundary worth documenting, and our prior data says it will concentrate at small scale.

## Endpoints

Primary, all deterministic: **existence rate** (share of cited cases that resolve in the oracle), **quote-fidelity rate** (share of quoted passages verbatim-verified against source text, using the calibrated strict band), and in the loop arm, **revision convergence** (rounds to a fully-verified draft, and whether one is reached within the round cap).

Secondary, jury-scored: **relevance rate of real citations**, for each surviving citation, does it actually support the proposition it is attached to? Scored by the order-debiased jury (both display orders, order-consistent verdicts only), because relevance is the one endpoint that cannot be read off a database, and unmitigated LLM judging is position-noisy at every scale we tested. The H2 substitution claim lives in the joint movement of the primary and secondary endpoints: existence rising while relevance falls is substitution; both rising is honest repair.

Tertiary, descriptive: citation count per draft (to see honest retreat), argument length, and where fabrication concentrates (our post-mortem found interpolated fabrications cluster in circuit reporters, 26.8%, versus 4.2% in U.S. Reports, worth confirming under pressure).

Two additions from the Phase 0 reading pass (see `../papers/positioning_notes.md`). **Abstention is a logged outcome in every cell**: LegalCiteBench showed models assert concrete wrong citations rather than abstain in over 94% of failures, but our stakes condition is exactly the manipulation that could change refusal behavior, and fabrication rates computed only over produced citations would confound the two. And adjudication uses **three labels, not two**, verified, fabricated, unverifiable/out-of-coverage, because the recurring killer in the prior literature is coverage gaps masquerading as fabrication; the unverifiable bucket gets a stratified manual audit and a sensitivity reanalysis.

## Known instrument limits, stated up front

The oracle's existence check was calibrated at 96.3% on curated human citations; its behavior on model-generated citations at scale is exactly what the pilot must confirm before freeze. Vendor citations (WL/LEXIS) are unresolvable by design and are excluded from the fabrication numerator, following the rule worked out in the gated-evolution project. Agency-reporter citations were a known blind spot of the checker (zero coverage in the post-mortem); the design must either extend coverage or scope claims to court-reporter citations, and the choice gets made and recorded before freeze, not after seeing results. Quote checking against pre-OCR-era sources runs into the wild-text problem (65.8% strict on old judicial prose), so temporal-restriction conditions must not silently conflate "older authority" with "worse OCR", the quote endpoint may need era-stratified reporting.

## Statistical commitments (draft)

Paired designs throughout: every condition contrast runs on the identical case-side task, so the case's identity, which the previous project showed dominates outcome variance, cancels within pairs. Fabrication-rate contrasts use cluster bootstrap at the draft level (citations within a draft are not independent). The two-model consistency rule is a gate on claims, not a statistic: a direction that appears in one family and reverses in another is reported as model-specific, never as the phenomenon. Zero-count cells use the rule of three, learned the hard way. Exact tests, corrections, and the minimum detectable effect at the planned n go into the frozen version once the pilot yields real per-draft citation counts.

## What this project promises not to do

No outcome prediction anywhere, nothing in this design touches partyWinning, so the memorization trap that shaped the last two projects does not apply. No LLM judge is used for any deterministically checkable property. No headline from one model. No post-hoc condition additions without a ledger entry. And no spending beyond Phase 0 without an explicit, per-phase go.
