**ARGUMENT**

A criminal defendant lacks standing to challenge a federal statute on Tenth Amendment grounds. Standing is a threshold jurisdictional requirement that must be resolved before any merits inquiry. *Steel Co. v. Citizens for a Better Env’t*, 523 U.S. 83, 94-95 (1998). A plaintiff must demonstrate an injury in fact that is concrete and particularized, fairly traceable to the challenged action, and redressable by a favorable decision. *Lujan v. Defenders of Wildlife*, 504 U.S. 555, 560-61 (1992).

The Tenth Amendment reserves to the States powers not delegated to the United States. Private litigants may not assert it. In *Tennessee Elec. Power Co. v. Tennessee Valley Auth.*, 306 U.S. 118 (1939), the Court held that private utility companies “absent the states or their officers, have no standing in this suit to raise any question under the [Tenth] Amendment.” *Id.* at 144. That holding controls here.

Five circuits have applied this rule to deny private-party standing to raise Tenth Amendment challenges. *United States v. Hacker*, 565 F.3d 522, 525-27 (8th Cir. 2009); *Oregon v. Legal Servs. Corp.*, 552 F.3d 965, 971-72 (9th Cir. 2009); *Brooklyn Legal Servs. Corp. v. Legal Servs. Corp.*, 462 F.3d 219, 234-35 (2d Cir. 2006); *Medeiros v. Vincent*, 431 F.3d 25, 33-36 (1st Cir. 2005); *United States v. Parker*, 362 F.3d 1279, 1284-85 (10th Cir. 2004). These decisions rest on the principle that the Amendment protects state sovereignty, not individual rights. *See also Brecht v. Abrahamson*, 507 U.S. 619, 635 (1993) (States possess primary authority over criminal justice).

Bond’s conviction under 18 U.S.C. § 229 therefore presents no justiciable Tenth Amendment claim. The statute was enacted pursuant to the Treaty Power and Necessary and Proper Clause. *Missouri v. Holland*, 252 U.S. 416, 432 (1920). Any federalism objection belongs to the States, which may vindicate their interests through political processes or direct litigation. *Medeiros*, 431 F.3d at 35. Allowing a convicted defendant to litigate structural claims would convert the Tenth Amendment into a personal defense, contrary to *Tennessee Electric* and the jurisdictional mandate of *Steel Co.*

The judgment of the court of appeals should be affirmed.
