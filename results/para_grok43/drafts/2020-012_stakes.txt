ARGUMENT

The Railroad Retirement Act provides that “[a]ny claimant” may obtain judicial review of “any final decision of the Board.” 45 U.S.C. § 355(f). The Board’s refusal to reopen Salinas’s 2006 application is such a decision.

Section 355(f) confers jurisdiction over “final” Board actions that terminate administrative proceedings. See Roberts v. R.R. Retirement Bd., 346 F.3d 139, 140 (5th Cir. 2003) (“Under the plain language of § 355(f), the jurisdiction of the federal courts of appeals is limited to the review of Board decisions on the merits of a claim for benefits after administrative appeals have been exhausted.”). The hearing officer’s determination that the four-year reopening window had closed, 20 C.F.R. § 261.2, ended the administrative process and left no further avenue for relief. That order is therefore final.

Califano v. Sanders, 430 U.S. 99 (1977), does not compel a different result. There the Court construed 42 U.S.C. § 405(g), which authorizes review only of a “final decision of the Secretary made after a hearing.” Id. at 108. The RRA contains no parallel limitation to decisions “made after a hearing.” Because Congress omitted that restrictive phrase, the jurisdictional inquiry turns solely on finality. See Szostak v. R.R. Retirement Bd., 370 F.2d 253, 254 (2d Cir. 1966); Sones v. R.R. Retirement Bd., 933 F.2d 636, 638 (8th Cir. 1991).

The Board’s own regulations confirm that reopening decisions are adjudicated on the administrative record and are not subject to further internal review. Once the hearing officer ruled, the agency’s position was fixed. Treating that ruling as unreviewable would insulate from judicial scrutiny an agency determination that directly affects a claimant’s statutory entitlement—an outcome at odds with the review provision Congress enacted.

The Fifth Circuit’s contrary holding rests on Roberts, which adopted the view of four other circuits without examining the textual differences between § 355(f) and § 405(g). That precedent should be overruled. The Board’s denial of reopening is a final decision subject to review under § 355(f).
