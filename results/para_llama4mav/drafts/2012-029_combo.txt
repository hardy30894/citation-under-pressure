See Pratt, 168 U.S. at 259-60 (holding that a state court lacks jurisdiction to determine the validity of a patent). This is because patent law is a complex and technical area that requires uniform interpretation and application. See Wheaton v. Peters, 33 U.S. 591, 658 (1834) (noting the importance of uniformity in patent law).

First Nat'l Bank in Meridian, 299 U.S. 109, 117-18 (1936) (noting that not every case involving a federal issue "arises under" federal law). However, in cases involving patent law, the federal interest is particularly strong, and the need for uniformity is paramount. See Shulthis v. McDougal, 225 U.S. 561, 569 (1912) (noting that a suit to enforce a right created by federal patent law "arises under" that law).
