ARGUMENT

The district court erred in granting summary judgment. Summary judgment is proper only when the record reveals no genuine issue of material fact. Celotex Corp. v. Catrett, 477 U.S. 317, 322 (1986); Anderson v. Liberty Lobby, Inc., 477 U.S. 242, 248 (1986). All reasonable inferences must be drawn in favor of the nonmovant. Matsushita Elec. Indus. Co. v. Zenith Radio Corp., 475 U.S. 574, 587 (1986).

I. Burnett supplied adequate FMLA notice.

An employee need not expressly invoke the FMLA. Collins v. NTN-Bower Corp., 272 F.3d 1006, 1008 (7th Cir. 2001). Notice is sufficient when it apprises the employer of a serious health condition and the probable need for leave. Price v. City of Fort Wayne, 117 F.3d 1022, 1026 (7th Cir. 1997). Burnett told Polo of his “weak bladder,” upcoming blood tests, elevated PSA, and appointments with a urologist. He compared his symptoms to his brother-in-law’s prostate cancer and supplied a physician’s order and biopsy description. These communications “provide[d] enough information to put the employer on notice” of a potentially serious condition. Aubuchon v. Knauf Fiberglass GmbH, 359 F.3d 950, 953 (7th Cir. 2004). Habitat’s subsequent grant of leave on January 6 and 8 confirms it understood the request. See Byrne v. Avon Prods., Inc., 328 F.3d 379, 382 (7th Cir. 2003).

II. Burnett supplied adequate ADA notice and request for accommodation.

Under the ADA, notice is adequate when the employee conveys both the disability and the desire for accommodation. Bultemeyer v. Fort Wayne Cmty. Schs., 100 F.3d 1281, 1285 (7th Cir. 1996). Burnett’s disclosures of prostate-related testing and biopsy needs, coupled with his request to transfer shifts for better restroom access, satisfied this standard. EEOC v. Sears, Roebuck & Co., 417 F.3d 789, 803 (7th Cir. 2005). Once notified, Habitat was obligated to engage in the interactive process. Id. at 805. Its failure to do so precludes summary judgment.

The district court’s contrary conclusion rests on an unduly demanding notice requirement unsupported by these authorities. Reversal is required.
