The government bears the burden of proving beyond a reasonable doubt that a defendant was a member of a conspiracy during the relevant statute of limitations period. When a defendant presents evidence of withdrawal from a conspiracy prior to the statute of limitations period, the burden remains with the government to prove that the defendant was still a member of the conspiracy during the relevant period. See United States v. Lothian, 976 F.2d 1257, 1261-62 (9th Cir. 1992); United States v. Grimmett, 236 F.3d 452, 455 (8th Cir. 2001). The D.C. Circuit's decision affirming the convictions of Calvin Smith and his co-defendants is therefore in line with established precedent, as it held that the government met its burden of proving the defendants' involvement in the conspiracy during the relevant period.
