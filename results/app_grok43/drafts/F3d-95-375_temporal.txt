The district court exceeded its authority by striking appellants' expert witnesses for noncompliance with deposition deadlines. See Hammond Packing Co. v. Arkansas, 212 U.S. 322 (1909). The remaining experts' testimony was admissible under governing standards for opinion evidence. See Frye v. United States, 293 F. 1013 (D.C. Cir. 1923). Summary judgment was therefore improper, as the record presented genuine issues of material fact. See Sartor v. Arkansas Natural Gas Corp., 321 U.S. 620 (1944). Appellants' notice of appeal was effective, the order having become final as to them. See Cohen v. Beneficial Industrial Loan Corp., 337 U.S. 541 (1949).
