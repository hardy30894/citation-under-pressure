**ARGUMENT**

Section 1254a(f)(4) provides that a TPS recipient “shall be considered as being in, and maintaining, lawful status as a nonimmigrant” for purposes of adjustment under § 1255. That statutory command satisfies § 1255(a)’s requirement of inspection and admission.

The INA defines “admission” as “the lawful entry of the alien into the United States after inspection and authorization by an immigration officer.” 8 U.S.C. § 1101(a)(13)(A). When Congress grants TPS, it places the alien in lawful nonimmigrant status. Because nonimmigrant status is available only to those who have been inspected and admitted, the grant of TPS supplies the requisite admission. See I.N.S. v. Cardoza-Fonseca, 480 U.S. 421, 432 (1987) (“where Congress includes particular language in one section of a statute but omits it in another section of the same Act, it is generally presumed that Congress acts intentionally and purposely in the disparate inclusion or exclusion”).

This reading is confirmed by the structure of § 1255. Subsection (k) permits adjustment when an alien “is present in the United States pursuant to a lawful admission” and has not failed to maintain lawful status. The two requirements are distinct; the first is satisfied by the TPS grant itself. Treating the phrases as interchangeable would render one superfluous, contrary to Hibbs v. Winn, 542 U.S. 88, 101 (2004).

The Third Circuit’s contrary conclusion—that status and admission remain categorically separate—overlooks that Congress expressly equated TPS with nonimmigrant status “for purposes of adjustment of status under section 1255.” 8 U.S.C. § 1254a(f)(4). That equivalence supplies the admission § 1255(a) demands. The decisions of the Sixth and Ninth Circuits correctly recognized this textual command. See Flores v. U.S. Citizenship & Immigration Servs., 718 F.3d 548, 553-54 (6th Cir. 2013); Ramirez v. Brown, 852 F.3d 954, 960-61 (9th Cir. 2017).

Petitioners therefore meet the threshold eligibility requirement of § 1255(a). The judgment of the court of appeals should be reversed.
