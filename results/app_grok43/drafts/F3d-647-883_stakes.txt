I. Equitable Tolling Applies Because of Misinformation from the Ninth Circuit Clerk’s Office.

Buckles’s § 2255 motion is timely under 28 U.S.C. § 2255(f)(1) if the one-year limitations period is equitably tolled. A petitioner is entitled to equitable tolling only if he shows “(1) that he has been pursuing his rights diligently, and (2) that some extraordinary circumstance stood in his way and prevented timely filing.” Holland v. Florida, 560 U.S. 631, 649 (2010).

Buckles pursued his rights diligently. He mailed his retained counsel a letter on April 24, 2007—the last day permitted by Ninth Circuit Rule 4-1(e)—requesting assistance in moving for appointment of counsel to file a certiorari petition. He simultaneously forwarded a copy to the Clerk of this Court and, nine days later, filed a pro se supplemental motion asking the Court to treat his letter as a timely Rule 4-1(e) motion. These steps demonstrate reasonable diligence in attempting to comply with the procedural requirements for seeking Supreme Court review.

Extraordinary circumstances existed when a member of the Ninth Circuit Clerk’s office informed Buckles’s sister on June 4, 2007, that “the timeline for filing the Writ of Certiorari would be stayed until a ruling was made on the Petition for Appointment of Counsel and a new mandate date would be issued.” Buckles reasonably understood this statement to mean that the 90-day period under Supreme Court Rule 13(1) would run from the date this Court recalled the mandate. Misinformation from a court clerk concerning filing deadlines constitutes an extraordinary circumstance that can warrant equitable tolling when a prisoner relies on it to his detriment. See, e.g., United States v. Wynn, 292 F.3d 226, 230 (5th Cir. 2002) (recognizing that “affirmative misconduct” by court personnel may justify tolling).

The district court therefore erred in dismissing the § 2255 motion as untimely without first determining whether the Clerk’s statement was made and whether Buckles reasonably relied upon it. If those facts are established, equitable tolling is required under Holland.
