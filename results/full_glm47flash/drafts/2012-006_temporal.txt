**Argument**

The government bears the burden of proving every element of a conspiracy charge beyond a reasonable doubt. *United States v. Perez*, 218 U.S. 111, 115 (1910). This burden includes proving the duration of the conspiracy and the defendant's participation during that time. *Id.* at 116. The burden of proof never shifts to the defendant to prove they withdrew from the conspiracy or that the conspiracy ended before the statute of limitations expired. *Krulewitch v. United States*, 339 U.S. 247, 251 (1950). Here, the government failed to prove that the conspiracy continued into the valid statute of limitations period. Consequently, the D.C. Circuit erred by shifting the burden of proof to the defendants.
