**Argument**

The post-removal-period statute does **not** authorize indefinite detention beyond the 90-day removal period. 8 U.S.C. § 1231(a)(6) permits detention only for a period reasonably necessary to secure an alien’s removal, not for life when removal is not reasonably foreseeable. The Supreme Court so held in *Zadvydas v. Davis*, 533 U.S. 678, 689, 699-700 (2001), construing the statute to avoid serious constitutional concerns.
