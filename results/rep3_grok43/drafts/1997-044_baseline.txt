**ARGUMENT**

Section 523(a)(2)(A) excepts from discharge “any debt … for money, property, services, or an extension, renewal, or refinancing of credit, to the extent obtained by … actual fraud.” 11 U.S.C. § 523(a)(2)(A). The phrase “to the extent obtained by” limits nondischargeability to the sum actually procured through the fraud. In re Levy, 951 F.2d 196, 198 (9th Cir. 1991). Treble damages awarded under the New Jersey Consumer Fraud Act are not compensation for money obtained by fraud; they are a statutory penalty imposed after the fact. Id. at 198-99. They therefore fall outside the statutory exception.

This Court has repeatedly held that exceptions to discharge must be construed narrowly to effectuate the Bankruptcy Code’s “fresh start” policy. See Grogan v. Garner, 498 U.S. 279, 286-87 (1991) (quoting Local Loan Co. v. Hunt, 292 U.S. 234, 244 (1934)). Allowing punitive or statutory multiples to survive discharge would enlarge the exception beyond its text and “contravene the policy of the Bankruptcy Code.” In re Levy, 951 F.2d at 198.

The contrary view, adopted below, rests on In re St. Laurent, 991 F.2d 672 (11th Cir. 1993). That decision treats the entire judgment—including punitive components—as a single “debt” arising from fraud. Id. at 679. But the statute does not except “any debt arising from fraud”; it excepts only the portion “obtained by” fraud. The St. Laurent reading erases the limiting clause Congress inserted. See In re Levy, 951 F.2d at 198.

The fraud finding itself does not alter the analysis. The bankruptcy court determined that Cohen’s implied representation of lawful rent constituted actual fraud under § 523(a)(2)(A). Even assuming that determination is correct, it establishes only that the $31,382.50 in overcharges was obtained by fraud. The additional $62,765 in treble damages was not obtained by fraud; it was created by operation of state law after the fact. In re Levy, 951 F.2d at 198.

Because the treble damages are not “obtained by” actual fraud, they are dischargeable. The judgment below should be reversed to that extent.
