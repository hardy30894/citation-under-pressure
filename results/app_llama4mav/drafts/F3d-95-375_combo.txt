However, as the Court noted in Glasser v. United States, 315 U.S. 60, 80 (1942), "the test of admissibility is not whether the evidence is conclusive, but whether it is relevant and has probative value." In this case, the testimony of Dr. Schroeder and Dr. Watson was relevant and had probative value, and the district court erred in excluding it.
