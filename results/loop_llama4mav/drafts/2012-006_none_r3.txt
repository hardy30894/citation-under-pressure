United States, 353 U.S. 391 (1957), the Court held that the government must show that the conspiracy continued into the limitations period, and that the defendant's participation in it was not limited to a period outside the limitations period (Id. at 396-397).

United States, 332 U.S. 539 (1947), where the Court reiterated that a defendant's participation in a conspiracy must be proven beyond a reasonable doubt, and that the government must show that the conspiracy continued into the limitations period (Id. at 556-557).
