The government bears the burden of proving every element of a conspiracy, including its duration. *United States v. Feola*, 420 U.S. 671, 687 (1975). When a defendant withdraws from a conspiracy, the conspiracy ends for that defendant. *United States v. Jimenez*, 50 F.3d 1071, 1077 (9th Cir. 1995). Consequently, the government must prove that the defendant remained a member of the conspiracy after the withdrawal. *United States v. Perez*, 8 F.3d 549, 553 (2d Cir. 1993).

Here, the government failed to meet this burden. The leaders of the conspiracy, Rodney Moore and Kevin Gray, split up before the statute of limitations period. Because the conspiracy had already fractured, the government could not prove that Smith and Raynor were part of a single conspiracy during the relevant period. Therefore, the district court erred in denying the motion for a new trial.
